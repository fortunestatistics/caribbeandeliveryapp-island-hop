# IslandHop Onboarding + Marketing Rollout Plan

## Brand Positioning Anchor
IslandHop is a **Caribbean-first super app** for **food, grocery, pharmacy, courier, taxi, and car rental**.

## Messaging Pillars
1. Caribbean-first convenience
2. Growth for local businesses
3. Income opportunities for drivers
4. One app, many services

## Recommended Rollout Order
1. Finalize brand messaging
2. Build driver onboarding content
3. Build merchant onboarding content
4. Produce photo/video library
5. Turn assets into social, website, and sales material

## First Production Batch
- Brand overview video (1)
- Driver onboarding video (1)
- Merchant onboarding video (1)
- Vertical social clips (10-15)
- Photo library (drivers, merchants, app-in-use, island lifestyle)
- Static graphics (checklists, FAQs, benefits cards, how-it-works)

## First Deliverables
- Driver onboarding deck
- Merchant onboarding deck
- Social media launch pack
- Website/landing page content
- WhatsApp/shareable flyer versions
