# Merchant Onboarding Content Kit

## Why Partner With IslandHop (Core Story)
Partner with IslandHop to access more customers, streamline delivery logistics, control operations, improve visibility, and grow faster with a Caribbean-first platform.

## Merchant Partner Series (Module Outline)
1. **What IslandHop offers businesses**
   - Demand generation + local discovery
   - Order and fulfillment workflow support
   - Shared logistics network
2. **Who can join**
   - Restaurants
   - Grocery stores
   - Pharmacies
   - General merchants and service businesses
3. **Information and documents needed**
   - Business owner details
   - Business contact and location details
   - Licensing and compliance documents as applicable
   - Banking/payout details
4. **How onboarding and approval work**
   - Submit application
   - Verification review
   - Approval and storefront activation
5. **How orders and fulfillment work**
   - Receive orders
   - Set prep/ready timing
   - Handoff and completion tracking
6. **Payments, commissions, and support**
   - Settlement visibility
   - Commission structure alignment
   - Ongoing merchant support workflows

## Merchant FAQ Sheet
1. **Who can apply?**
   - Restaurants, grocery, pharmacy, and qualified local merchants.
2. **What is needed to get approved?**
   - Complete business profile, required documentation, and payout setup.
3. **How are orders managed?**
   - Through merchant workflow tools for acceptance, prep, and handoff.
4. **How do settlements work?**
   - Through platform-defined payout cycles and business wallet/payment flows.
5. **How do I get support?**
   - Merchant support channels are available for setup and live operations.
