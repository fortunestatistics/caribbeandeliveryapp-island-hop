# Driver Onboarding Content Kit

## Why Drive With IslandHop (Core Story)
Drive with IslandHop to earn flexibly across multiple service types in one app. Complete fast onboarding, receive wallet payouts, manage live order flow, use proof of delivery for trust, build ratings, and unlock performance incentives.

## Driver Welcome Series (Module Outline)
1. **What IslandHop is**
   - Caribbean-first super app for food, grocery, pharmacy, courier, taxi, and car rental
   - One platform connecting customers, merchants, and drivers
2. **How to sign up**
   - Create account
   - Complete profile and territory details
   - Start onboarding steps
3. **Documents needed**
   - Driver's license
   - Vehicle registration
   - Insurance certificate
   - Certificate of character
   - Profile photo
4. **How deliveries work**
   - Receive request
   - Accept and navigate to pickup
   - Verify pickup and complete delivery
   - Upload proof of delivery where required
5. **How earnings and payouts work**
   - Per-job earnings
   - Incentive boosters and eligibility
   - Wallet balance and payout timing
6. **Safety, support, and performance**
   - Driver safety best practices
   - Customer communication standards
   - Ratings, cancellation discipline, and service quality
   - Support escalation paths

## Driver FAQ Sheet
1. **How long does approval take?**
   - Most applications are reviewed after all required documents are submitted.
2. **Can I choose what jobs I accept?**
   - Yes, drivers can manage accepted requests within platform rules.
3. **How do payouts work?**
   - Earnings are reflected in-app via wallet and payout workflows.
4. **What affects my rating?**
   - Timeliness, professionalism, communication, and delivery accuracy.
5. **How do I get support?**
   - Use in-app support/help channels for order and account issues.
