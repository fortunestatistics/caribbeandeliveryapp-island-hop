<product_requirements>
The goal is to build IslandHop, a comprehensive Caribbean delivery app offering Food, Pharmacy, Groceries, Courier, Taxi, and Car Rental services, featuring a Caribbean-themed UI. Core functionalities include real-time order tracking with driver chat, AI customer support, and multiple payment methods (including Stripe).

The application requires robust onboarding systems:
1.  **Drivers**: 5-step process (Personal Info, Vehicle Info, Documents, Banking, Review).
2.  **Restaurants**: 5-step process (Business Info, Operating Hours, Documents, Banking, Review).
3.  **General Businesses/Suppliers**: A 6-step flow for various categories with detailed business, operating, document, banking, and review steps.

Implemented features also include:
*   Comprehensive KPI tracking accessible via a dashboard.
*   Mobile-responsive UI with a hamburger menu.
*   Dedicated customer order forms for Taxi, Courier, Pharmacy, and Groceries.
*   Driver and Business Earnings Dashboards for payment transparency.
*   User authentication (login/signup), subscription plans, and restaurant menu browsing.
*   Full backend integration for authentication, payment gateways, real-time updates (WebSockets), and maps.
*   A visually appealing main landing page, resembling DoorDash/Uber.
</product_requirements>
